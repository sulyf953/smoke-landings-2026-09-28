Stop gut-pausing Meta creatives — a 30–45m weekly kill ritual

If you pause ads by feel, you either kill winners early or keep bleeders running.

  1. Export the last 7–30 days from Ads Manager (or your creative performance report)
  2. Rank by spend first, then look at CTR, frequency, and conversions
  3. Kill on capital exposure — multiples of your CPA — not “it’s been live 3 days”
  4. Same session, queue the next test so the account never goes empty
