Method · Meta Creative Kill Sheet

Weekly Meta creative kill ritual that stops gut-pausing

If you pause by feel, you either kill winners in learning or keep bleeders running. This is a 30–45 minute Ads Manager pass with capital-exposure rules — no new SaaS seat.

  1. HOLD (first 72h): Creative age under 72 hours? Do not judge ROAS / CPA / CVR yet — learning noise wins fake fights.
  2. KILL (capital exposure): Spend already ≥ 2× your target CPA and 0 purchases? Pause. Slide 1.5× if aggressive, 3–4× if patient / high AOV.
  3. FATIGUE FLAG: CTR down ≥15–20% vs this creative’s 7d peak and frequency ~2.5–3 and CPA rising → iterate or kill; don’t “hope it comes back.”
  4. DIAGNOSE before you blame the ad: CPM↑ + CTR flat = auction · CPM flat + CTR↓ = creative · CTR good + no CV = landing / offer.
  5. FEEDER: Keep ~20% budget in a test campaign; promote winners; don’t reset learning daily with endless micro-edits.

Tune floors to your margin and AOV. Halal-safe examples only. Not affiliated with Meta. Not financial advice.
